## The idea in one sentence

A function is a small named program that takes data, does something with it and gives back a result.

## An everyday comparison

Think of a **blender**: you put fruit in (the input), press the button and juice comes out (the output). You don't need to know how the motor works to use it.

## What you will learn here

- What lambdas and currying are, explained without fear.
