## The idea in one sentence

An artificial neuron is a tiny calculator that takes numbers, gives each one an importance (weights) and decides how much it "lights up".

## An everyday comparison

Imagine deciding whether to go out and play: you check if it's sunny, if you finished your homework and if your friends are outside. Each thing **weighs** differently in your decision. A neuron does exactly that with numbers.
